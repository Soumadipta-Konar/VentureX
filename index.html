Grading & Valuation Methodology Transparency MoEFCC BWMR 2022

🏛️ Category 1: Sourced / Regulatory Mandates

  • Battery Waste Management Rules (BWMR) 2022: Notified by MoEFCC, Govt of India. Mandates Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR) credit trading for battery producers and recyclers.
  • EPR Credit Valuation Mechanism: Platform calculates estimated EPR credit value per kWh transferred for certified second-life or material recycling, rewarding higher recovery chemistry (NMC > LFP).
  • Second-Life Battery Safety Standards: Refers to Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) and AIS-156 safety requirements for second-life pack re-certification.

🧪 Category 2: Illustrative / Calibrated Parameters

  • Degradation Physics Constants: Cycle degradation exponents ($\alpha$), calendar aging rates (%/yr), and Arrhenius thermal decay coefficients are empirical values calibrated to published literature (NREL/IEEE).
  • Benchmark Prices (₹/kWh): Informal scrap baselines (~₹1,450/kWh), New Lead-Acid (~₹5,200/kWh), and New Lithium (~₹12,500/kWh) are market-average estimates (not sourced from a single proprietary company dataset).
  • Cycle Estimation Ratio: Km-to-cycle conversion factors (2W: 40 km/cycle, 3W: 55 km/cycle, 4W: 120 km/cycle) reflect nominal real-world range assumptions.

Mathematical Degradation Model Formulation

SOH% = 100 - [ 20 × (N_cycles / N_nominal)^α × (1 + 0.6 × (DoD% - 80)/100) + (Cal_rate × Age_yrs × e^(0.03 × (Temp°C - 25))) ]
